# Brag Plan: Waynora

## What is this app?

Waynora is a multi-agent AI travel advisor that orchestrates specialist research agents to produce comprehensive, honest travel itineraries — no fake progress, no fabricated results.

## The angle

The product's own honesty is the hook. Waynora doesn't pretend travel planning is instant. It tells you "5–15 minutes" and means it — and gives you a 2048 game while you wait. The video should feel like a premium product film that respects the viewer's intelligence.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The landing page hero fades in — the Waynora wordmark and the headline "AI trip planning — tell us where you want to go." The luxury editorial aesthetic is immediately visible. Then a quick cut to the chat interface where a user starts typing a travel request.

## Key moments (the middle)

1. **The chat interface** — User types a travel request, submits it. The waiting shell appears with elapsed time and the 2048 game.
2. **The waiting experience** — The 2048 game is playable, the elapsed clock ticks, dynamic messages rotate. This is the product's differentiator.
3. **The result** — A comprehensive travel itinerary appears in Markdown, well-formatted and detailed.

## Outro / punchline

The Waynora logo lands with the tagline "Deep-research travel intelligence with total transparency." Fade to black.

## User flow worth showing

Entry → Key action → Result:
1. User lands on the landing page, sees the value proposition
2. User signs in, navigates to chat, submits a travel request
3. User waits with the 2048 game, then receives a detailed itinerary

## Tone

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film
- Interpretation: Confidence through restraint. Slow, elegant transitions. Let the product speak. No flashy gimmicks — the luxury aesthetic is the visual identity.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080

## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)

- Background: Deep warm dark (near-black with warm undertones)
- Accent: Gold/amber (the brand's primary accent)
- Text: Warm white/cream
- Display font: Serif (editorial, luxury feel)
- Body font: Clean sans-serif
- Strongest visual element: The landing page hero with the Waynora wordmark and the waiting shell with 2048

## Share copy (draft)

Waynora: AI trip planning that actually researches. No fake progress, no fabricated itineraries — just deep multi-agent research and an honest 5–15 minute wait with 2048 to keep you company.

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed
- Music: A warm, confident, slightly cinematic track. Not too upbeat — more "premium product launch" than "startup hype."
- Music treatment: Start at moderate volume, duck slightly under the final logo, fade out at the end.
- SFX posture: sparse — a soft click on the submit button, a subtle tile-merge sound in the 2048 game, a gentle whoosh on transitions.
- Audio-coupled moments: The 2048 tile merge could have a subtle sound. The itinerary reveal could have a soft "arrival" cue.
- Restraint rule: Audio must not feel like a corporate explainer video. Keep it warm and confident.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Landing Page Hero — 4s

The landing page loads. The Waynora wordmark is centered, the headline "AI trip planning — tell us where you want to go" is visible. The luxury editorial design is immediately apparent — warm dark background, gold accents, serif typography.

Sequential/interaction: none — this is a static hero shot with a slow zoom-in.
Audio intent: Establish the premium feel. Music begins.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: Warm, confident bed begins.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Sign In — 3s

The sign-in page appears. The user enters credentials and signs in. The form is clean, minimal, elegant.

Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate typing in the email field, then the password field, then clicking sign in.
Audio intent: Build momentum. The user is taking action.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle key ticks as text is typed, a soft click on sign in.
Music: Continues, slightly more present.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Chat Interface + Waiting — 6s

The chat interface appears. The user types a travel request: "Plan a 5-day trip to Jaipur, Rajasthan in December." They hit send. The waiting shell appears — elapsed time starts, the 2048 game is visible and playable.

Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate typing the travel request, hitting send, then the waiting shell appearing. Show the 2048 game with a few tiles.
Audio intent: The product's differentiator moment. The waiting experience should feel engaging, not frustrating.
Audio-coupled idea: typing sounds for the request, a soft "send" click, then the 2048 tile sounds as the game is played.
Music: Continues, perhaps a slight swell as the waiting shell appears.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Itinerary Result — 5s

The waiting shell disappears. A comprehensive travel itinerary appears in the chat — well-formatted Markdown with day-by-day plans, accommodation suggestions, transport options, and activities.

Sequential/interaction: yes — the itinerary appears as if it's being generated, with sections appearing one by one.
Audio intent: The payoff. The user gets their result.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft "arrival" cue as the itinerary appears.
Music: Swells slightly, then begins to duck.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Logo Outro — 2s

The Waynora logo appears on a clean background with the tagline "Deep-research travel intelligence with total transparency."

Sequential/interaction: none — static logo with a subtle glow.
Audio intent: Land the brand.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: Ducks under the logo, then fades out.
Transition mood: fade to black.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, confident, premium
**Audio summary:** A warm cinematic bed that builds gently through the user journey, swells at the itinerary reveal, and fades under the final logo.
