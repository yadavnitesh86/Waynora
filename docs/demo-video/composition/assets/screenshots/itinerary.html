5-Day Jaipur Itinerary

December 15–19, 2026 · 2 travelers · Budget: ₹45,000

Day 1 — Arrival & Old City
Morning

Arrive at Jaipur Airport (JAI). Pre-paid taxi to Samode Haveli (30 min, ₹450). Check in and freshen up.

Afternoon

Explore the Pink City on foot: Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar. Lunch at Lassiwala (₹300/person).

Evening

Sunset at Nahargarh Fort (entry ₹200). Dinner at 1135 AD (₹1,200/person) — reserve ahead.

Day 2 — Amber Fort & Culture
Morning

Amber Fort (entry ₹500/person). Arrive by 8 AM to avoid crowds. Explore Sheesh Mahal and Ganesh Pol.

Afternoon

Jaigarh Fort (entry ₹300). Lunch at The Rajput Room (₹800/person).

Evening

Albert Hall Museum (entry ₹400). Evening bazaar: Johari Bazaar for jewelry and textiles.

Day 3 — Pushkar Day Trip
Full Day

Day trip to Pushkar (11 km, 45 min by taxi). Visit Brahma Temple, Pushkar Lake. Lunch at Sunset Café (₹400/person). Return by 6 PM.

Day 4 — Crafts & Relaxation
Morning

Sanganer block-printing workshop (₹1,500/person, 2 hrs). Visit Anokhi Museum.

Afternoon

Spa afternoon at Samode Haveli (₹2,500/person). Pack for departure.

Day 5 — Departure
Morning

Breakfast at hotel. Last-minute shopping at Bapu Bazaar. Airport transfer by 11 AM.
